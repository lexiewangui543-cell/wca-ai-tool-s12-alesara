Sample Study Buddy Session

Date: 11 November 2026
Project: WCA AI Study Buddy

Conversation

Student: What is cybersecurity?

Study Buddy: Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks and data from digital threats.

Student: What is phishing?

Study Buddy: Phishing is a cyberattack where someone uses deceptive messages to trick people into revealing sensitive information.

Student: Give me an example.

Study Buddy: A fake email pretending to come from a bank and asking someone to verify their account details.

Session Summary

The student learned the meaning of cybersecurity and phishing, including a practical example.
